# Curated KC

**Your Passport to Kansas City.**
*Curated for the Journey. Built for the World.*

A mobile-first digital passport experience connecting Kansas City 2026 Football visitors to handpicked local collaborators, exclusive offers, and thoughtfully mapped destinations across Kansas City.

---

## About This Project

Curated KC is a digital passport guide built for the Kansas City 2026 Football season by Vagabond Mercantile LLC. The platform connects international and domestic visitors with local Kansas City businesses through exclusive passport perks, a curated matchday trail map, and a boutique travel guide experience.

The site is designed to feel like a boutique concierge platform and elevated city passport, not a tourism board or coupon site.

**Live Site:** [curatedkc.co](https://curatedkc.co)

## How to Update the Site

All content lives inside `index.html`. To make updates:

1. Open the GitHub repository
2. Click on `index.html`
3. Click the pencil icon to edit
4. Make your changes
5. Click Commit changes

GitHub Pages will automatically rebuild and push the update live within 60 seconds.

To update the map, replace `map.svg` with a new SVG file of the same name.

## Brand Guidelines

**Primary brand language:**
Curated for the Journey. Your Passport to Kansas City. Eat. Explore. Experience KC. Repeat. Go where the locals go.

**Preferred football phrasing (avoid direct World Cup trademark):**
Kansas City 2026 Football, Matchday, Summer Football in KC, Global football energy
